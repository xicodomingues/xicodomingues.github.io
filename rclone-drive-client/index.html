rclone Drive client

This is a personal OAuth client that one person uses with rclone to back up and restore their own files in their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

  • The app accesses Google Drive only to read and write files in the signed-in owner's own Drive.
  • It collects, stores and shares no user data. Nothing is sent anywhere except between the owner's computer and Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's security settings.
